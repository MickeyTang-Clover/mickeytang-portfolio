## Physics and My Interests

Astronomy is one of my main interests. I have taken PHYS 1020Q, PHYS 2701, and PHYS 2702 at UConn. These classes helped me learn more about astronomy and astrophysics. I also have my own telescope, an Explore Scientific FirstLight 100 mm Maksutov-Cassegrain. I use it to observe the Moon, planets, and other objects in the night sky. Using a telescope also helps me connect what I learn in class with real observations. I hope I can keep learning more about astronomy in the future.
